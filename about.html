Our Story

Nasa Histoires is a Colombian alternative music band that originated in Tunja, Boyacá, Colombia. The band's story began around 2015–2016 when a group of university friends discovered a shared passion for music and decided to create something of their own. The original members included Daniel Pinto, Víctor Cely, Santiago Jiménez Palomino, Daniel Tovar and Jair Serrano. While studying at university, they became interested in different musical styles and began exploring ways to combine them. One of their biggest inspirations came from a university class about French culture and folklore, where they discovered gypsy jazz, particularly the music of Django Reinhardt. Fascinated by its rhythms and melodies, they started experimenting with blending these European influences with Colombian and Latin American music, creating a sound that would eventually become their musical identity.

The band's name, Nasa Histoires, reflects this combination of cultures and their desire to tell stories through music. The name brings together the word Nasa, which they associated with the meaning of "those" in their naming process, and Histoires, the French word for "stories." Together, the name can be understood as "Those Stories" or "Aquellas Historias." It represents their intention to transform emotions, experiences and everyday situations into musical stories that listeners can connect with. Their music developed into a mixture of alternative rock, indie, jazz, folk and Latin American influences, giving them a distinctive sound that balances energetic instrumentals with thoughtful and sometimes melancholic lyrics.

As the band continued developing, its members began working toward releasing their first album. However, after graduating from university in 2019, life became more complicated. The members started pursuing different careers and taking regular jobs, including work in education and engineering. Eventually, the group separated, with everyone moving toward different opportunities and projects. Despite this, they still had music they wanted to share with the world. In 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, they reunited to release their debut album, Aquellas Historias, on November 25. The album represented the beginning of their musical journey and brought together the stories and emotions they had been developing throughout their time as a band.

Meet the Band

Daniel Pinto

Daniel Pinto is the lead singer and one of the primary creative forces behind Nasa Histoires. He is responsible for writing many of the band's lyrics, which frequently draw inspiration from personal experiences, emotions and everyday struggles.

Instagram
Víctor Cely

Víctor Cely plays the saxophone and contributes significantly to the band's jazz-inspired sound. His musical background includes an interest in folklore and orchestral music. He also studied foreign languages alongside Daniel Pinto, which helped introduce them to French culture and gypsy jazz.

Instagram
Santiago Jiménez Palomino

Santiago Jiménez Palomino, often credited as Santiago Jiménez, is the band's drummer. He provides much of the rhythmic foundation behind their music, helping create the energetic sound that contrasts with the band's more melancholic lyrics. He has experience playing different styles, including alternative rock, grunge and soul. He is also associated with other musical projects, including Black Bufon and La Mairé.

Instagram
Daniel Tovar

Daniel Tovar is the band's bass player. His instrument provides the underlying basslines that connect the drums, guitar and other melodic instruments. He contributes to the band's overall arrangements and collaborative songwriting process.

Instagram
Jair Serrano

Jair Serrano is the band's guitarist and contributes to its musical arrangements and creative direction. He brings knowledge of different musical styles, including jazz, soul and rock. His guitar work helps give Nasa Histoires its distinctive sound, combining alternative rock with jazz influences and Colombian musical elements.
