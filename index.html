News About Us

Rolling Stone

Nasa Histoires is a Colombian band that was born in 2016 when friends Daniel Pinto, Victor Cely, Santiago Jiménez Palomino, Daniel Tovar and Jair Serrano decided to form a project to express their feelings through art. Thus they created a group with a fusion of interesting sounds and a duality of melancholy and joy.
The band "is a journey through different latitudes, emotions, colors, shapes," as described by its member Daniel Pinto in a conversation with ROLLING STONE in Spanish, in which they talk about their most recent tour of Mexico, their recent album FLORA, their history and much more.

Billboard Colombia

His extremely cheerful songs have been a kind of therapy, both for the group and those who listen to it. The secret is in its introspective lyrics that connect with thousands of followers. "We worked on a concept that without looking for it found us," emphasizes Daniel Pinto, singer of Nasa Histoires, the group based in Tunja, which became one of the contemporary musical phenomena with the greatest virtual presence in the country. Or at least that's what it seemed at the end of last year, when after a concert of his in Bogotá, many content creators made videos on networks about the experience lived in a show of the group. Pinto refers to the fact that his compositions, which combine jazz manouche, rock and folklore, are a party, but they also talk about very personal issues and at times they can feel melancholic: "We doubt very much whether to release these songs, for example with 'Cactus' I felt a little intimidated, because it was like saying 'this is who I am, this is what I feel. We were very scared and asked ourselves: 'do people want to hear this?'"

Caracol Radio

Daniel Pinto, vocalist and composer of the songs, explained on CARACOL RADIO that the song they have just presented, "Girasol", is a reference to the toxic relationships between some human beings, such as the parable of the flower that we cut from a garden because we fall in love with its beauty, despite being aware that by cutting it we condemn it to wither and die, losing that attractiveness that we loved. He says that in human relationships sometimes we fall in love with someone who we consider that his way of being is appropriate for us and then we modify the essence that made us fall in love. Dani Pinto also talked about how they found metaphorical messages going to names of flowers or related to the flora, hence the name of the album that will become the second in the history of Nasa Histoires.
